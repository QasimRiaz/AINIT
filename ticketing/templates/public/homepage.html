The problem

Alert storms, siloed tools, and slow root-cause analysis.

Modern networks generate millions of signals. NOCs and SRE teams spend their time clearing false positives, copy-pasting data into tickets, and guessing which device actually caused the impact.

  • PRTG, SNMP traps, NetFlow, and cloud metrics all speak different languages.
  • Critical insights are buried in chat threads and historic tickets.
  • Runbooks live in wikis no one opens during an incident.
  • Human context is needed, but engineers are overloaded.
